## Summary

The cardholder states they did not take part in a card-not-present transaction. The merchant can respond when the purchase was authenticated, or when there is strong evidence that the cardholder (or someone they allowed) made it.

## What wins

Identity Check authentication shifts the liability to the issuer. If there was no authentication, the merchant should connect the purchase to the cardholder: matching AVS and CVC2, plus a history of earlier, undisputed orders from the same device or network.

## Digital goods

For goods delivered by email or download there is no delivery address to compare. Device and account history become the only link to the cardholder, so a brand new account with no history usually cannot be defended.

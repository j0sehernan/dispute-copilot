## Summary

Mastercard groups several cardholder complaints under 4853. In this project we use it for goods that were not as described or arrived defective.

## What wins

The merchant shows that the cardholder was offered a return or replacement and did not follow through, and that the goods are still with the cardholder.

## What does not win

A received return with no refund. In that case the merchant should accept and refund.

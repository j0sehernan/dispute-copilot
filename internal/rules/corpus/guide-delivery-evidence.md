title: Delivery evidence
---
## Good delivery proof

A carrier event with status delivered, a timestamp and the delivery address, plus a signature name or a photo of the parcel at the door.

## Weak delivery proof

A label creation event, "shipped" or "in transit". Delivery to an address that differs from the order, even by one house number, works against the merchant.

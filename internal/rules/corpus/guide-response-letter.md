## Tone

Neutral and factual. No blame on the cardholder, no marketing language. Address the issuer's dispute team.

## Structure

One opening line with the case number, reason code and amount. One paragraph per piece of evidence. A closing line that asks for the chargeback to be reversed.

## Personal data

Use only the data needed to identify the transaction: case number, transaction reference, card last four digits, order number. Do not include full card numbers or internal notes.

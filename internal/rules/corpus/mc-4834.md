## Summary

4834 covers processing errors at the point of sale. Here we use it for duplicate charges.

## What wins

Evidence that the two transactions are two purchases: separate order IDs and separate fulfillment.

## When to accept

When the same order was captured twice. A refund of the duplicate before the dispute is the only other defense.

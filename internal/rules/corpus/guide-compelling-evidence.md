## Principles

Every statement in a response should point to a record: a transaction field, an order, a carrier event or a customer message. Issuers read many packages a day; short, specific facts work better than long explanations.

## Order of the package

Start with the claim and the reason code, then the evidence that answers the claim directly, then the supporting history. Put the strongest single fact first.

## Missing records

If a record is missing, say so. Do not fill gaps with guesses. A package that claims something the issuer can check and disprove loses credibility for the whole case.

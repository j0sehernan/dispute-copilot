## Summary

The cardholder expected a credit after a return or cancellation and did not get it.

## What wins

The refund record (date, amount, ARN) issued before the chargeback. Without a refund, the merchant needs proof that no return was received.

## When to accept

The goods came back and no refund was made. Accept the chargeback and do not send a separate refund, or the cardholder is paid twice.

## Response window

Each notice has a respond-by date set by the acquirer. It is usually shorter than the network limit because the acquirer needs time to forward the case. Missing it means the chargeback is accepted by default.

## Late refunds

A refund issued after the dispute was opened does not cancel the chargeback and can lead to paying the cardholder twice. Once a dispute exists, resolve it through the dispute, not with a new refund.

## Summary

The cardholder paid but says the goods were not provided. The merchant must prove delivery to the cardholder's address.

## What wins

Tracking that ends in "delivered" at the order's shipping address, plus a signature or photo. Dates matter: the delivery must happen before the cardholder opened the dispute.

## When to accept

If the parcel is still in transit, lost, or was delivered somewhere else, accepting and opening a claim with the carrier is usually cheaper than a representment that will fail.
